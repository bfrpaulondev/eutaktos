# Governance

## Product authority

Product decisions must preserve the core boundaries in README.md, particularly:
- no automated spiritual qualification;
- human confirmation for sensitive decisions;
- privacy/data minimization;
- free access and donation-only funding model;
- interoperability and data portability.

## Required review areas

Changes affecting any of the following require explicit architecture/security review:
- authentication;
- authorization;
- encryption;
- Review Center;
- AI tool access;
- imports/exports;
- third-party messaging;
- map/location data;
- backups;
- data retention/deletion;
- cross-congregation features.

## Transparency

Material architecture and privacy decisions should be documented as ADRs. Security-sensitive implementation detail may be intentionally excluded from public documentation when disclosure would increase operational risk.
