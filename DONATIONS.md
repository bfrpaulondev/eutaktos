# Donations

Eutaktos is intended to be free to use.

If donations are enabled, the following rules apply:
- completely voluntary;
- no subscriptions;
- no premium features;
- no higher limits for donors;
- no priority access based on donations;
- no ads;
- no sale of congregation/user data;
- payment/donor records separated from congregation records;
- donation providers receive no congregation-management data beyond what is strictly necessary for the donation transaction;
- public transparency about the purpose of donations and major infrastructure costs.

Donations support sustainability; they do not purchase access, influence, privileges or product decisions about individual congregation members.
